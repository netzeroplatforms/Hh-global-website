Awaab's Law changed everything. In December 2020, two-year-old Awaab Ishak died in Rochdale from a respiratory condition caused by prolonged mould exposure in his social housing. The subsequent coroner's verdict and the Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023 created a legal landmark.

Since December 2024, every registered social landlord must investigate damp and mould within 14 days, fix emergency hazards within 24 hours, and non-emergency repairs within 7 days. Every failure is now directly actionable.

Claims have grown from approximately 6,500 per year in 2019 to over 50,000 today — and rising. Every case requires independent scientific evidence.
