Eight critical questions + decision logic

The eight burden-shifting questions

What to listen for: Named consultant, submission stage, completion date.

Red flag: "We know we need to do it" OR expectation that BridgePoint will handle it.

If red flag: This is a gating item. Pause until ownership is clear.

What to listen for: Signed agreement from PHIN Consulting PL to PHIN MED UK, available immediately.

Red flag: "It is being sorted" OR any hesitation OR vagueness.

If red flag: This is a diligence killer. Do not proceed without clear documentation.

What to listen for: Named developer, sprint date, committed delivery.

Red flag: "It is on the backlog" OR no developer assigned.

Why it matters: This unlocks the 12,500-person community pilot. Everything downstream depends on it.

What to listen for: "Semi-automated clinical decision support, not fully automated image classification." Clear framing of what the algorithm does and does not do in production.

Red flag: Overclaiming image AI capability. Vague language about automation.

Why it matters: Overclaiming kills credibility with NHS and acquirers. Get the framing right now.

What to listen for: Written closure evidence. All prior agreements terminated. No residual equity claims outstanding.

Red flag: Ambiguous settlement OR unresolved legacy claims.

Why it matters: This is a standard diligence question any buyer will ask. Clarity now saves trouble later.

What to listen for: Active application calendar. Specific timelines. Named PIs if NHS applications are in train.

Red flag: "No bandwidth" OR "Not aware of these programmes."

Why it matters: Grants are the fastest non-dilutive funding route. Missing this is leaving money on the table.

What to listen for: Named NHS trust (lead site). Dated ethics submission plan. Protocol ready or in final draft.

Red flag: "Protocol exists but nothing has moved" OR no named site.

Why it matters: This is the evidence engine for everything downstream. It needs to be in motion.

What to listen for: Specific position or milestone-based structure. Clear numbers.

Red flag: "We can discuss that later" OR deferred terms while strategic value is extracted now.

Why it matters: You need to know the ask before committing time and capital. Get it explicit.
