From Strategy to Audit-Ready.

A message from Sam White on the launch of HH Global Holdings' complete laboratory deployment system.

SW
Sam White
Chief Executive Officer, HH Global Holdings

When I talk to organisations standing up regulated laboratory capability, I hear the same story. The lab gets built twice.

First on a budget. Then again, painfully, when the auditors arrive and the documentation, traceability, and quality systems don't hold up.

I have seen what that second build costs. Capital, time to revenue, and scientific reputation. Retrofitting ISO 17025/15189 onto a working laboratory is one of the most expensive mistakes in regulated science.

So at HH Global Holdings we built a better way, and today I am proud to launch it: LabArchitect™.

LabArchitect™ is our complete eight-module laboratory deployment system. It starts well before a site is even chosen, with feasibility, strategy, site selection, scoping and design, and runs all the way through to a validated, accreditation-ready laboratory, so compliance is built in from inception, not bolted on later.

We invert the usual sequence. We build the governance, validation, traceability, and reporting architecture before the lab opens, so accreditation becomes the natural endpoint of how the lab already operates, not a separate project.

One trusted partner, providing overall leadership and a single point of accountability, seamlessly coordinating specialist suppliers from strategy and infrastructure through to digital systems, quality control, and defensible reporting.

Here is what that gives you:

  • An audit-ready laboratory from day one, not six months after opening
  • Defensible methods and defensible data, in audit, in regulator review, and in court
  • Seamless coordination and management of trusted specialist partners, so the whole build stays aligned under a single point of accountability
  • Your scientists trained on their own SOPs and systems, not generic frameworks
  • A clear path to accreditation, where the submission pack is a by-product of how the lab already runs

I built LabArchitect™ for organisations that need their first lab to be their last. Built right, accredited cleanly, defensible from day one.

If you are planning a new laboratory, or rescuing one that is fighting its accreditation, I would like to hear from you.
