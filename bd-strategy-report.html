Four Client Personas: What They Buy & Why

Understanding what our existing clients actually buy is the foundation for replicating those relationships at scale.

Persona A — R&D Retainer Client

Example: Genetix Labs

SMEs or early-stage startups with a product concept and no in-house scientific resource. They need credentialled scientists who will execute the work, not just advise.

  • Systematic review and literature validation
  • Bench R&D — assay development, sample testing
  • Technical advisory on regulatory roadmap
  • Credibility-by-association with academic credentials

Deal pattern: Starts at £3–5K/month, grows as trust builds. Genetix Labs evolved into Sam holding equity.

Under-monetised: Not buying ISO validation, expert witness services, or diagnostics development yet.

Persona B — Drug Development Client

Example: Current preclinical CRO contract (1yr 4mo remaining)

Pharma or biotech companies with a compound needing to reach human trial readiness. They need a full CRO pathway but can't afford or don't want Big CRO overhead.

  • Preclinical study design and execution
  • Mouse model development
  • Biomarker identification and validation
  • Phase 1 trial readiness preparation

Why HH: One of the only organisations that can take a client from systematic review through R&D, validation, regulatory compliance, preclinical work, and Phase 1 under one roof.

Critical gap: This client is NOT being offered a companion diagnostic. Our multiplex LFT platform is the natural cross-sell.

Persona C — Forensic & Legal Client

Example: Emerging — Legal Futures talk drove 78% LinkedIn uplift

Solicitors, housing authorities, insurers, criminal defence and family law firms who require a credentialled, court-ready expert witness or independent scientific report.

  • Mould and environmental testing for housing litigation
  • Expert witness reports (EWS1/cladding, drug testing)
  • Forensic drug testing for criminal and family law
  • Independent scientific input for environmental cases

Why HH: Prof Wilson is Forbes 30u30, UK Government Committee on Toxicity adviser, and former NHS Chief Scientific Officer. Dr White spoke at Legal Futures — 78% LinkedIn uplift from 12 minutes on stage.

Volume potential: One law firm handling 200+ cases/year at £500–2,000 per instruction = £100–400K/year from a single client.

Persona D — Diagnostics Partner

Example: ALS Global (paused — re-engage via Sam); pipeline: Eurofins, SYNLAB, SGS, Quotient Sciences, Almac

Companies with a diagnostic idea, patent, or market need who lack wet lab capability to develop, validate, and seek regulatory approval.

  • Lateral flow test design and microarray development
  • Antibody and protein panel validation
  • IP co-development (our multiplex LFT is patent-pending and world-first)
  • Regulatory pathway strategy (MHRA, CE/UKCA, ISO 17025/15189)

Why HH: The multiplex LFT is genuinely novel — ALS Global was willing to fund £300K+ before the pause.

Deal pattern: £100K–£500K+ project-based. ALS model: exclusive licence + 10% revenue share.
