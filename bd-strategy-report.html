Four Client Personas: What They Buy & Why

Understanding what our existing clients actually buy is the foundation for replicating those relationships at scale.

Persona A — R&D Retainer Client

Example: Genetix Labs, CBD consultancy

SMEs or early-stage startups with a product concept and no in-house scientific resource. They need credentialled scientists who will execute the work, not just advise.

  • Systematic review and literature validation
  • Bench R&D — assay development, sample testing
  • Technical advisory on regulatory roadmap
  • Credibility-by-association with academic credentials

Deal pattern: Starts at £3–5K/month, grows as trust builds. Genetix Labs evolved into Sam holding equity.

Under-monetised: Not buying ISO validation, expert witness services, or diagnostics development yet.

Persona B — Drug Development Client

Example: Current preclinical CRO contract (1yr 4mo remaining)

Pharma or biotech companies with a compound needing to reach human trial readiness. They need a full CRO pathway but can't afford or don't want Big CRO overhead.

  • Preclinical study design and execution
  • Mouse model development
  • Biomarker identification and validation
  • Phase 1 trial readiness preparation

Why HH: One of the only organisations that can take a client from systematic review through R&D, validation, regulatory compliance, preclinical work, and Phase 1 under one roof.

Critical gap: This client is NOT being offered a companion diagnostic. Our multiplex LFT platform is the natural cross-sell.

Persona C — Forensic & Legal Client

Example: Emerging — Legal Futures talk drove 78% LinkedIn uplift

Solicitors, housing authorities, insurers, criminal defence and family law firms who require a credentialled, court-ready expert witness or independent scientific report.

  • Mould and environmental testing for housing litigation
  • Expert witness reports (EWS1/cladding, drug testing)
  • Forensic drug testing for criminal and family law
  • Independent scientific input for environmental cases

Why HH: Prof Wilson is Forbes 30u30, NHS CSO, government committee advisor. Dr White spoke at Legal Futures — 78% LinkedIn uplift from 12 minutes on stage.

Volume potential: One law firm handling 200+ cases/year at £500–2,000 per instruction = £100–400K/year from a single client.

Persona D — Diagnostics Partner

Example: ALS Global (paused); pipeline: Historon

Companies with a diagnostic idea, patent, or market need who lack wet lab capability to develop, validate, and seek regulatory approval.

  • Lateral flow test design and microarray development
  • Antibody and protein panel validation
  • IP co-development (our multiplex LFT is patent-pending and world-first)
  • Regulatory pathway strategy (MHRA, CE/UKCA, ISO 17025)

Why HH: The multiplex LFT is genuinely novel — ALS Global was willing to fund £300K+ before the pause.

Deal pattern: £100K–£500K+ project-based. ALS model: exclusive licence + 10% revenue share.

Emerging Pipeline Discussions

Three early-stage discussions now underway. Light outreach is beginning. These inform the website and messaging development as much as near-term revenue.

Women's Health

Hysteron — Menopause Diagnostics

Hysteron is developing a menopause blood test. They are currently using a commercial laboratory and are seeking a more advanced R&D partner capable of multi-analyte diagnostics and clinical validation.

  • Persona match: Diagnostics Partner (Persona D)
  • HH angle: Our multiplex lateral flow platform (20–60 analytes, 3 drops of blood) is directly applicable to a multi-marker menopause panel. Hormonal biomarkers — FSH, LH, estradiol, AMH — are exactly the multi-analyte problem our technology solves
  • What they need from us: R&D capability, multi-analyte validation, regulatory pathway (CE/UKCA), potential IP co-development
  • Website implication: Ensure women's health and menopause diagnostics are visible in Human Health messaging
  • Estimated value: £100K–£300K R&D contract + potential licensing

Next step: Schedule introductory call. Bring the multiplex LFT one-pager and ask what analytes their current commercial lab cannot measure simultaneously.

Population Health

Andrew Roddam is the former CEO of Our Future Health — the UK's largest health research programme, recruiting 5 million participants to accelerate the development of new ways to predict, prevent, and treat diseases. He is now available and looking for new engagements.

  • Persona match: Strategic Partner / Persona B (Drug Development) at scale
  • HH angle: Our wet lab capability, biomarker development expertise, and diagnostic platforms are precisely what a population health programme needs for translational work. Philippe's NHS CSO role and One Health framework are directly relevant

Next step: Introductory call. Frame as a strategic collaboration — not a client engagement. Explore what Andrew is building next and where HH's wet lab and diagnostic capability creates value for him.

UAE · Animal Genomics

UAE Contacts — Camel Genomics

UAE-based contacts with interest in camel genomics. Camel racing, breeding, and health is a multi-billion dirham industry in the Gulf. Genomic profiling and diagnostic capability for camelids is a significant and underserved market.

  • Persona match: Market Enterer (Persona D) + Diagnostics Partner
  • HH angle: Dr White's background in applied immunology and allergen/biomarker diagnostics translates directly to veterinary genomics. Philippe's government advisory experience and One Health framework provide credibility at national scale
  • What they need from us: Genomic diagnostic development, camelid biomarker panels, potential UAE lab capability or oversight
  • Website implication: Camelid and high-value species genomics should be visible in the Animal Health section alongside equine

Next step: Discovery call to define scope — is this genomic profiling for racing performance, disease detection, or breeding? Each has a different commercial and regulatory pathway. Confirm who the counterparty is and whether there is government or family office backing.

Messaging note across all three: None of these are confirmed clients yet — they are early discussions. The website and outbound messaging should position HH broadly enough to attract these profiles without overstating the relationship. Use the language of capability and approach, not client lists.
