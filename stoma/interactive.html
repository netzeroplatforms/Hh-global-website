Commercial Target Audience

Three distinct segments. Each needs different value prop, entry route, revenue model, and timeline.

Audience 1: Stoma Bag Suppliers

⚡ Fastest Route to Revenue · 6–9 Months
Coloplast, ConvaTec, Salts, Hollister. They spend millions acquiring patients. Stoma Alert threatens that. That threat is why they'll pay.

Why They Buy

  • Commercial Threat: Stoma Alert recommends competitor products if they don't engage. Patient switching risk.
  • Data Value: Real-world patient complication data, product usage patterns worth millions to R&D.
  • NHS Leverage: Independent clinical tool gets NHS adoption their branded apps cannot.

Revenue Models

  • Partnership Fee: £30–100K annual for preferred supplier status
  • Data Licence: £20–50K annual for anonymised market intelligence
  • Acquisition: £5M+ exit. Buys IP, ontology, patient relationships, UK deployment

Audience 2: NHS / Integrated Care Boards

🏥 Highest Credibility · Longest Timeline · 18–24 Months
NHS contracts create the credibility that unlocks manufacturer premium pricing and acquisition valuation.

Why They Buy

  • £8,000 cost saving per prevented hospitalisation — direct budget impact
  • Reduces stoma nurse workload — NHS staffing pressure
  • Aligns with NHSE digital-first, remote monitoring priorities
  • 165,000–205,000 UK patients = material population health impact

Entry Pathway

  1. SBRI Healthcare Phase 1 application (£100K, 6 months) — funded gateway
  2. Kent & Medway ICB innovation meeting — proven digital adopter
  3. ASCN UK demo — nurses decide what NHS adopts
  4. SBRI Phase 2 or NIHR i4i PDA — funded NHS deployment
  5. NHS pilot agreement — the proof point for manufacturer premium

Audience 3: Patients (Direct & Community)

👥 Evidence Engine, Not Revenue Engine
Community first, subscription second. Every patient interaction generates data that makes every other commercial conversation more powerful.

Primary Patient Needs

  • Leak detection — higher priority than infection monitoring
  • Social acceptance — psychological support post-surgery
  • Community connection — peer support, not just clinical
  • Expert access — rapid clinical response without travel

Acquisition Channels

  • Facebook group (12,500 members) — pilot access via Clare Pascoe
  • Colostomy UK newsletter — warm relationship, high-intent audience
  • Ileostomy Association (IA) — 50-year nurse network
  • Hospital discharge packs — QR code, point of highest need
  • Charity support groups and local events
