Most laboratories fail ISO 17025 audit not because the science is wrong — but because compliance was bolted on after the fact. LabArchitect is HH Global's structured laboratory deployment system: eight integrated modules that build governance, infrastructure, workflows, data systems, and quality management in sequence, from day one. The result is a laboratory that produces valid, traceable, defensible results — and is audit-ready without expensive rework.

The Eight Modules
01

Laboratory Governance

Organisational structure, authority matrix, impartiality framework, and data protection controls — the compliance foundation before a single test is run.

02

Infrastructure & Resources

Lab layout, zoning, environmental controls, equipment specification, procurement, installation, calibration schedules, and asset management.

03

Validated Workflow Engine

End-to-end workflow design, SOPs, method verification and validation, batch structure, throughput modelling, and process timing documentation.

04

Sample & Data Traceability

Sample ID schema, barcode systems, chain-of-custody processes, storage SOPs, and LIMS integration — full traceability from receipt to report.

05

Digital Lab Backbone

LIMS configuration, instrument-to-report data flow, data integrity controls, access permissions, audit trails, and full system validation.

06

Quality Control & Assurance

QC framework, acceptance criteria, statistical monitoring, trend analysis, and continuous assurance of result validity across all workflows.

07

Defensible Reporting Suite

Standardised report templates, result interpretation frameworks, conformity and decision rules — outputs aligned to regulatory and legal standards.

08

ISO Management System

Quality manual, SOP library mapped to ISO clauses, document control, internal audit programme, CAPA processes, and management review framework.
