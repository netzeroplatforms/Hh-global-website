The Problem

⏱️

45-90 Day Approval Waits

Installers complete a site survey, then submit G98/G99 applications via PDF forms and emails to one of 12 different DNOs. Approval takes 6-13 weeks — and installers can't close deals without approval certainty.

📋

Paper-Based Process

Each DNO has its own forms, submission methods (email, portal, web form), and compliance requirements. No standardised API. No unified platform. Installers spend hours per application on admin.

💷

£100-300 Per Application

Installers pay third-party companies like G59 Projects ~£100 per application to handle the paperwork. For busy installers doing 10+ applications per month, that's £1,000-3,000/month in purely administrative costs.

📉

Lost Deals & Customer Anxiety

Customers can't get same-day confidence on whether their installation will be approved. 45-90 days is enough time for them to change their mind, find a cheaper competitor, or lose interest entirely.
