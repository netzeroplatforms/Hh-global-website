Compliance Standards & DNO Requirements

    

      

        

          
Engineering Recommendations — G98 vs G99

          

            
G98 applies to systems≤ 3.68kW per phase

            
G99 required above3.68kW (single) / 11kW (3-phase)

            
G98 notification window28 days

            
G99 application window45 days (Part A) / 65 days (Part B)

            
G99 Part B threshold> 50kW — full technical assessment

            
Deemed approval routeG99 11–50kW if headroom >60%

          

        

        

          
Protection & Safety Standards

          

            
Loss of Mains (LoM)Required — all systems

            
Anti-islandingRequired — all grid-tied systems

            
EREC G83Micro-generation connection guide

            
BS 7671 18th EditionWiring regulations compliance

            
DNO protection relay testG99 only — witnessed test

            
CT meteringG99 Part B >50kW

          

        

      

      

        

          
Certifications & Financial Schemes

          

            
MCS CertificationMandatory for SEG eligibility

            
Smart Export Guarantee (SEG)Available — G98/G99 compliant systems

            
SEG rate (typical)5–15p/kWh (supplier dependent)

            
VAT on solar installations0% (residential, since Apr 2022)

            
Inverter complianceIEC 62109 / CE marked

            
Battery safety standardIEC 62619 / UL 9540

About Headroom

    
Know your grid capacity before you apply. Built for UK solar installers, by people who understand the DNO problem.

    

      

        
⚡ The Problem We Solve

        
The UK solar industry loses millions of pounds annually to DNO application delays. Installers submit applications blind — without knowing whether a substation has headroom, whether export limitation will be imposed, or which application route (G98/G99) applies. The result: 23–67 day delays, rejected applications, and lost sales.

      

      

        
🎯 The Headroom Difference

        
Headroom maps real network capacity against installation profiles before a single form is submitted. Know your application route, your export limitation risk, and your approval timeline in seconds — not weeks. Eliminate wasted submissions, accelerate conversions, and give customers confidence before they commit.

      

      

        
📡 What This Tool Does

        
Enter a postcode or DNO region, system size, battery configuration and EV setup. Headroom calculates: application route (G98/G99), export limitation risk, grid impact score, predicted timeline, compliance checklist, and annual generation + savings projections. Everything an installer needs — before the paperwork starts.

      

      

        
🚀 Roadmap

        
Phase 2: Live substation capacity data via ENA API. Phase 3: Automated G98/G99 application pre-fill and submission. Phase 4: Installer CRM integration with application status tracking. Phase 5: API access for solar software platforms and CRMs.
