Why this thread wins the primary push
Run-rate this quarter

HM3 Legal pre-positioned. Sam already authoritative in the legal-fraternity audience. Tracy + Mark have spent the last week on the BD pipeline build.   1. Frame the offer as Environmental Expert Witness, not Awaab's Law product.   4. Sam's Awaab's Law content cadence opens 27 May. First three posts queued in advance; cadence runs against the legal-fraternity audience. Each post passes the three-filter check (no FungID specifics; no Fungid customer solicitation; pre-May-2025 capability set).

  6. Construction / materials route as the upstream play. Eradication at source — Mark's third sub-route in the Roddam call. Long-cycle, high-margin, complementary to the litigation pipeline.
