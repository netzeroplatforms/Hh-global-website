The strategic asset: Business Plan v3.0

Beyond the individual workstreams, the engagement has produced a single high-value asset: a rebuilt group business plan that gives HH an investor-ready and partner-ready narrative it did not have before. What the rewrite delivered:

  • A clear identity. Repositioned HH from a fragmented CRO / consultancy into a translational science and evidence platform, taking science from biomarker discovery to validated diagnostics, therapeutics and regulator-ready evidence, molecule to deployment, with no handoff gaps. This is the language Sam signed off on 29 May.
  • The group structure, brought forward and made legible. The parent and its integrated companies (Research & Innovation, Data & Evidence Services, Insight) articulated up front, with the historic consulting entity structurally separated from the regulated vehicles.
  • The product and IP portfolio foregrounded. ArrayDx (patent-pending, trademark submitted) and PatchDx (patent-pending) elevated as the growth story, alongside HH’s own ISO-aligned Nottingham laboratories (including an ISO 7 controlled clean room).
  • Company accolades separated from individual credentials, so the institutional story stands on its own.
  • The commercial case framed: a serviceable opportunity in the order of £300 to 600M across the priority UK and adjacent-Europe vectors, and a modelled pathway to £5 to 10M run-rate over five years from a £25K+ MRR / nine-engagement base (figures being finalised with HH).
  • Delivered as a fully tracked rewrite responsive to the 29 May Sam and Tracy feedback: every change visible and reasoned, so the team can see exactly what moved and why.
