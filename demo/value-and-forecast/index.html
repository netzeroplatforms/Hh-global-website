Part One

Phase 2: forecast, targets and mutual accountability

The build phase produced a named pipeline. This half puts indicative values, probabilities and timings against it, brings the key people to life with a specific ask against each, and proposes a shared scorecard that holds both BridgePoint and HH to account for converting it.

How to read the figures. Each value is a range, Low / Base / High, anchored to published comparables (public grant and contract awards, sector deal data, procurement benchmarks; sources held on file). Probability is our judgement of conversion within the stated window. Weighted value = Base × probability, the prudent planning number. Three things are kept separate so nothing is double counted: (1) services revenue HH could invoice, (2) capital and strategic events such as investment, licensing or acquisition, which are balance-sheet not revenue, and (3) connector value, the pipeline a relationship can generate rather than revenue in its own right. All figures are GBP and exclude VAT.
