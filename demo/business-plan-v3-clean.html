Executive Summary, HH Global Holdings

HH Global Holdings is a translational science and evidence platform. Built on a One Health framework spanning human, animal and environmental health, we take scientific work from biomarker discovery through to validated diagnostics, novel therapeutics and regulator-ready submission. We combine our own laboratories, genomics and multiplex diagnostics, bioinformatics, regulatory intelligence and evidence systems under one accountable group, accredited to ISO 17025 and 15189 standards. We operate through three integrated companies, HH Research & Innovation, HH Data & Evidence Services and HH Insight, with shared scientific leadership and shared IP, so development runs from discovery to deployment with no handoff gaps. We are not a consultancy, a sequencing house, or a generalist CRO. We are the partner that turns science into validated, defensible and commercially deployable diagnostics, therapeutics and evidence.

To convert that platform into recurring revenue, HH operates an integrated offer comprising:

  • Research & Innovation , preclinical and translational research in our own laboratories: diagnostics development across patent-pending platforms, assay development, and novel therapeutic discovery through to preclinical and clinical work.
  • Data & Evidence Services , converting complex research into defensible, auditable scientific conclusions: systematic reviews, evidence synthesis, and regulator-ready and court-ready evidence packages.
  • Insight: regulatory strategy and ISO 9001 / 13485 / 15189 / 17025 quality management, productised in the LabArchitect™ eight-module turn-key lab deployment system.

This integrated architecture is positioned to capture concurrent market opportunities, such as the un-served multiplex point-of-care diagnostics segment, the choked UK ISO accreditation pathway for small diagnostics labs, and, as a supporting evidence channel, the regulated damp and mould enforcement market. The Group serves pharma and biotech, the NHS, diagnostics and healthtech companies, veterinary, agri-health, international health ministries, and research-driven SMEs. We translate science into validated, deployable outcomes rather than treating One Health as a research label.

The Group enters this plan with £25K+ MRR and nine active engagements as of May 2026. The serviceable opportunity across the four highest-priority vectors is in the order of £300–600M in addressable annual UK and adjacent-Europe spend. HH's protected intellectual property, integrated cross-sector capability, and first-mover position in the One Health CRO category provide a clear pathway to capturing a £5–10M annual run-rate revenue opportunity within a five-year horizon, with a £1.2M run-rate established within twelve months on the base case.

Opportunity

Problem Worth Solving

Executive Summary (for investors and pitch presentations):

HH Global Holdings addresses the structural fragmentation of the UK contract research market: the lack of an integrated provider capable of taking a client problem from biomarker through preclinical and translational research, into ISO-validated diagnostics and therapeutics, through regulatory submission, and on to defensible, regulator-ready evidence. Current alternatives force buyers to assemble a syndicate of specialists across multiple disparate suppliers, with no single point of accountability, no scientific continuity, and significant integration overhead. HH consolidates this pathway into a single Group, replacing handoff cost with a unified scientific platform.

Comprehensive Overview (for board members and clinical / commercial stakeholders):

Concurrent UK, European and Gulf (UAE, Saudi) market dynamics have created the commercial opening HH is built to take. First, the multiplex point-of-care diagnostics segment is growing at approximately 14% CAGR off a $10Bn+ global lateral flow base, yet no dominant commercial product exists at the 20 to 60 target per strip specification that HH's patent-pending ArrayDx platform delivers. Second, the UK ISO 17025 and 15189 accreditation pathways have become a structural choke point for the ~150 small diagnostics labs spinning up around regulated diagnostics, with no productised accreditation deployment service in market. Third, and as a supporting evidence channel rather than a growth pillar, regulated demand for defensible environmental and health evidence (including the damp and mould enforcement created by Awaab's Law) provides a credibility-led route that HH can serve without basing its income or development upon it.

The current alternatives, fragmented Big-CRO contracting, single-specialism consultancies, generalist law firms attempting to commission their own scientific evidence, and lab-build advisories sold by hour rather than outcome, exhibit a notable lack of integrated, accountable, cross-vector delivery. The result is delayed engagements, inconsistent evidence, overstretched in-house teams at SME diagnostics buyers, and rising claim and procurement costs across the affected market segments.

HH Global Holdings addresses these issues through three integrated operating subsidiaries delivering, in a single contractual envelope, the full scientific and evidential pathway from molecule to deployment. This integration aligns with the contemporary direction of UK life-sciences procurement and regulatory thinking, outcome-based purchasing, preventative care economics, evidence-led litigation reform, and the consolidation of accredited capability into fewer, more capable counterparties.

  • Independent expert witnesses (RICS, RSPH-credentialled mycologists, university-affiliated scientists) operating on individual instructions.

Limitations: Capacity-constrained, no lab capability, no productised offers, no cross-sell. Cannot underwrite multi-instruction frameworks or full-pipeline CRO programmes. Reputational risk where a single individual is single-point-of-failure to a litigation timetable.

  • Boutique mould-investigation firms: typically environmental-only, no scientific authorship for court submission.
