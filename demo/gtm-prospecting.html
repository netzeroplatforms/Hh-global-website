Across many damp and mould reports I have reviewed as an expert witness, the single most common failure is not in the testing. It is in one paragraph — the "alternative explanations" section. A judge does not need to be convinced that mould was present. They need to be convinced that the expert considered, and properly excluded, every other plausible cause of the tenant's harm — and that the methodology was capable of doing so.
