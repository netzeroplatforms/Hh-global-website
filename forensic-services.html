The Regulatory Landscape

Why Independent Forensic Analysis Matters

Following Awaab's Law (2023), social housing providers and tenants are legally required to address damp and mould within fixed timescales. This has triggered a litigation explosion, with solicitors, loss adjusters, and housing authorities requiring independent, court-admissible forensic evidence to support claims, remediation decisions, and regulatory compliance.

Our forensic services bridge the gap between environmental testing and legal standards, delivering scientific rigour that courts and regulators expect.
