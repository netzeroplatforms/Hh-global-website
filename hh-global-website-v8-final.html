Who We Are

Science-Led.
Evidence-Driven.

HH Global Holdings Ltd is a multidisciplinary international group operating across biotechnology, data science, regulatory systems, and asset management. We develop, operate, and support companies and programmes that deliver measurable impact in human health, animal welfare, agriculture, and environmental science.

Our approach is grounded in rigorous methodology — from systematic reviews and meta-analyses at the top of the evidence pyramid, through to preclinical drug development, genetics laboratory validation, and ISO 17025-accredited commercial testing. We bridge the gap between academic research and real-world application.

Our teams serve on government advisory committees, deliver clinical trials with partners such as AstraZeneca, contribute to international white papers, and have been recognised by Forbes 30 Under 30. We bring the same rigour whether we are advising on housing health policy following Awaab's Law, developing sublingual immunotherapies for equine sweet itch, or building diagnostics laboratories from the ground up.

Clinical / Commercial

Innovation & Intellectual Property

Patent-Pending Technologies

Multiple UK patent-pending technologies and platforms designed for rapid detection, targeted intervention, and applied health monitoring across clinical, veterinary, agricultural, and environmental settings.

Diagnostics & Therapeutics

Rapid diagnostic and therapeutic technologies enabling point-of-care detection and intervention across human, animal, and environmental health applications.

  • Multiplex Lateral Flow Test (UK Patent-Pending) — True multiplexing on a single strip: 20–60 individual proteins/antibodies per test from 3 drops of blood. Simultaneous antibody AND protein detection.
  • Micro-Needle Diagnostic Plaster (UK Patent-Pending) — Non-invasive plasma collection via micro-needle technology. Eliminates blood draw requirements. Transformative for point-of-care and culturally-sensitive settings.

Biotechnology Platforms

Assay and translational platforms enabling high-throughput biomarker identification, drug discovery, and translational development.

  • Microarray Biomarker Platform — Spotted microarray technology enabling ~1,000 different proteins readable from 3 drops of blood. High-throughput biomarker ID, allergen fractionation, drug target discovery.
  • Extended Patent Library — Active pipeline of patent applications spanning animal health diagnostics, agricultural testing platforms, and environmental monitoring systems. Sector-specific applications of core technologies.

Data & Health Intelligence

Data-driven systems and analytical approaches converting complex datasets into defensible insights across human, animal, and environmental health domains.

  • One Health Data Integration — Integrated analytical framework bridging human, animal, and environmental health data. Epidemiological modelling, One Health pathway analysis, integrated risk assessment.
  • Evidence Generation Workflows — Systematic approaches to evidence generation, validation, and regulatory documentation. Designed to support rapid translation from bench to bedside to regulation.

One Health. One Platform.

Our diagnostic, therapeutic, and analytical platforms translate seamlessly across human, animal, and environmental health without redesign. The same microarray technology that identifies allergens in horses detects pathogens in livestock, screens drug targets in humans, and tests soil microbiomes in agriculture. Multiplied commercial value. Unified scientific framework.

Applied Science

Vaccine Development

Collaboration with UAE partners to develop a brucellosis vaccine for camel populations — leveraging our diagnostics platform for biomarker identification, immune response monitoring, and preclinical validation in a One Health context spanning human and zoonotic risk.
